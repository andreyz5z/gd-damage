log.hint.starHits=the star «{0}» deals damage — we do not raise it as a buff; tick it in the list of presses, and it will strike on cooldown
log.hint.starReplacing=the star «{0}» is bound to «{1}» — it is a REPLACEMENT ATTACK: it is assigned to a button and strikes instead of the normal hit. We can count those, but in THIS run we do not strike with it — the save does not keep the assignment. Choose it in the "attack with" list, and the star will work
log.hint.starIdle=the star «{0}» is bound to the skill «{1}», and that one does NOT STRIKE in this run (not ticked for pressing and not in the weapon pool) — there are no activations at all, and it is not bad luck
