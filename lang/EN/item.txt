# THE ITEM TOOLTIP AND BONUS LINES (item tooltips, the "Total bonuses" tab).
#
# Most tooltip lines are the game's own templates in the chosen language. Here are our words: where
# the game has no template, and the fallback text for when there is no game or the tag was not found.
# The labels are in Title Case, as the game writes its own lines, so that they sit well next to them.

# DoT duration tail: "+31% Frostburn Damage with +45% Increased Duration". Normally the game's template
# (`ImprovedTimeFormat`); this text is used only when it is missing.
item.durationWith=with {0}% Increased Duration

# Damage type names in a conversion line ("50% conversion: Vitality Damage to Acid Damage").
# Normally taken from the game (`tagConversion<Type>`); these are the fallback. The game has no tag for bleeding.

item.conv.Fire=Fire Damage
